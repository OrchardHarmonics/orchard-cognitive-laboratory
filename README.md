## First milestone: The Missing Distinction

The engine computes a 32-situation, five-bit model. Case 00 has one safe report; Case 01 has one harmful report. Case 02 has two conflicting, distinct roots. The paper fixture assumes at most one false report across the whole context. The anchor consumes that allowance.

The local rule cannot label either target. A context-wide rule can warrant opposite point labels, but a receiving view hiding bit 0 still merges them. Exposing bit 0 alone also fails because the local rule remains unresolved. The two repairs together give two decisive receiving answers with unchanged evidence and compatible worlds.

Capture a current view, then try simulated execution. Permission can be withdrawn independently. Changes to evidence, provenance, revision, rule, view, assumptions, access or known coverage validity invalidate the captured binding. Execution recomputes receiving labels; it does not trust stored answers.

### Stress testing

- Error allowance 0 lies below the conflicting anchor’s feasibility floor: no world fits.
- Error allowance 2 permits target reports to be false: both target labels become unresolved.
- Removing the anchor likewise restores uncertainty.
- Disallowing exposure of bit 0 prevents the coupled receiving repair.
- A separate audit fixture can make Case 00 actually harmful while keeping its safe report. The reasoning engine does not receive hidden truth. Under allowance 1 the actual world is excluded even while the model remains internally consistent. A supplied trusted audit counts the two actual false reports and revokes coverage validity. Receiving execution then holds.
- Using the audited error floor changes the assumption to 2, includes the audited fixture, and restores uncertainty. This is a supplied assumption-repair demonstration, not acquired self-learning.

## Second milestone: Carry the Method Forward

A supplied source enquiry scaffold acquires a rule-construction procedure on an internal full-view witness with positive gain. The exact old policy subtree stays first. The method is loaded into a child with no observations, conclusions, receiving grants, receipts or archive. The loader checks scope, version and structural binding, replays acquisition/qualification, and requires a current scoped owner grant. This local binding is not a cryptographic signature or hostile-host certificate.

The receiving experiment supplies two new contexts, each with fresh evidence roots. The second reverses the target labels. The child invokes its construction policy on lawful internal views and acquires a compositor whose immutable captured basis must exactly match its current component policy. A checked view/rule transaction has positive final gain, preserves earlier labels and leaves evidence and compatible worlds unchanged. Intermediate effects emit no actions.

Nine matched routes expose the dependency:

| Route | Receiving acquisition rounds | Decisive decisions with fresh permission |
|---|---:|---:|
| Inherited method intact | 1 | 4/4 |
| Construction method removed | 1 (failed) | 0/4 |
| Removed, then restored | 1 | 4/4 |
| Reconstruct, one round | 1 | 0/4 |
| Reconstruct, two rounds | 2 | 4/4 |
| Fresh policy, two rounds | 2 | 4/4 |
| Unrelated enquiry removed | 1 | 4/4 |
| Composite already installed | 0 | 4/4 |
| Supplied direct search | 0 | 4/4 |

The first successful receiving context develops the method; the second reuses it with new evidence. Acquisition budget is shared across the two contexts. All routes use a common checked source/load fixture, then intervene on the active receiving policy; “fresh” discards that policy and starts from `hold`. Direct search bypasses the construction policy. Neither control proves that method import is universally required.

The live receiving permission starts off: successful repair gives four labels but zero permitted actions. Explicitly turn it on to simulate release/block and the reversed pair. The matched comparison has supplied fresh permission, clearly distinguished from live controls. The acquisition-budget plot compares 0/1/2 rounds under valid load/access and current permission. Stress probes independently test revoked load permission, inaccessible bit, invalid captured basis and a polluted bootstrap.

Source acquisition costs one separate common round. Loading replays that qualification. Receiving counters report candidates, constructor invocations, qualifier cases (including final capture), effective component uses, pair checks and final receiving checks. Acquisition rounds are not elapsed time or total compute. Failed rounds count. These are selected work counters, not an exhaustive machine-operation cost model.

Use **Run installed sequence** at the bottom to stack the modules. Stage one adds a separate qualified source acquisition to its fixed repair fixture and emits a versioned construction artifact. Stage two validates and consumes that artifact; it does not import the first stage’s reports, answers or receiving grant. Stage three checks stage two’s milestone record as provenance for its separate diagnostic experiment. The sequence does not alter live controls.

## Third milestone: Ask What Matters

The diagnostic world has four components, exactly two required, and an installed AND/XOR response library. This gives twelve expression–set pairs and six possible actions. Fourteen proper nonempty read-only queries are available. The policy receives responses and operational metadata; simulator truth is excluded from support reconstruction, query ranking and qualification.

An initial AND-only tree is supplied scaffolding. At its leaf, full AND/XOR support may still disagree about the required set. A supplied compiler ranks queries by separated different-action pairs, balanced world partitions, lower price and lower mask. Every represented world must reach its correct set before the constructed plan qualifies. Conventional active diagnosis starts from the whole library, providing a strong comparison.

At 18 unit-price runtime ticks, the exact toy census is:

| Route | Correct inside AND/XOR (12 cases) | Incorrect inside | Withheld inside | Incorrect in separate OR stress (6 cases) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial route plus constructive enquiry | 12 | 0 | 0 | 3 |
| Additional enquiry removed | 2 | 0 | 10 | 0 (all 6 withheld) |
| Conventional active diagnosis | 12 | 0 | 0 | 3 |

These are this implementation’s small deterministic populations, not a reproduction of the paper’s counts. Main and stress populations remain separate. Changing price and deadline recomputes comparisons. Unit query price is one operation unit; width price is selected component count. Each operation unit occupies a non-watch tick. Every third tick belongs to a continuing watch, and the entire episode window is traced after an early answer or a hold. Refresh, receipt qualification and receiving check each require one further operation unit. Development/qualification counters are separate; no physical concurrent scheduler or complete compute measure is implemented.

The guided experience and observation slider show support after each query. Receiving permission starts false. Query denial, refresh denial, missed watches, stale source versions, empty support, known coverage contradiction and insufficient budget each hold receiving use. Hidden simulator truth scores correctness independently.

The OR failure fixture produces model-unanimous wrong set `{0,3}` for actual set `{0,1}`. With fresh permission this is an incorrect simulated admission; without it, the wrong proposal is withheld. A separate trusted exact-set audit records the contradiction. Later use in that fixture holds without automatically installing OR or erasing the earlier admission. Expression acquisition is implemented separately in milestone four.

The runner validates stage two’s milestone envelope as provenance. It does not import its five-bit rule constructor into the different diagnostic model, or carry old observations or grants. Stage three’s compiler remains supplied.

## Fourth milestone: Learn What Was Missing

Stage four reconstructs §§4–4.1 of *Constructive Enquiry and Acquired Model Extension*. A supplied exact-set audit identifies four incorrect old-library proposals at n=4/5, c=2/3. The selector receives audited K and all 88 proper-query measurements; it receives no OR mechanism name. Its supplied 26-program grammar contains ≥, = and ≤ comparisons against 0,1,2,3,4,c,c−1, plus all residues modulo 2 and 3. Exactly one program survives: `s≥1`, where `s=|K∩q|`.

Selection and semantic validation are separate visible steps. The candidate is checked against all 3,100 distinct n=6/c=2,3,4 query measurements. These measurements were inspected during development, so they are not described as held-out runtime evidence. A replayed certificate binds the expression, evidence, grammar and finite scope. Installation leaves receiving permission unchanged. The interpreter, selector, grammar and enquiry compiler stay fixed.

A subsequent frozen census uses a fresh dimension n=7,c=3: 35 required sets under each of AND/XOR/OR. At 48 unit-price ticks with supplied permissions:

| Intervention | Correct / 105 | Incorrect | Withheld | Runtime operation units |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Retained expression | 105 | 0 | 0 | 1,008 |
| Expression removed | 71 | 34 | 0 | 828 |
| Exact expression restored | 105 | 0 | 0 | 1,008 |
| Conventional expanded diagnosis | 105 | 0 | 0 | 981 |

All 34 gains are OR cases. The 70 previously correct AND/XOR outcomes remain correct. For the visible actual set `{0,1,3}`, four common initial observations leave the old library proposing wrong set `{0,1,2}`. The enlarged library has 31 possible sets on those same observations. Five further questions resolve the correct set. An enlarged family can therefore expose a gap that a smaller family hides.

A separate 70-case stress census uses two uninstalled predicates, `s≥2` and `s=1`, with 64 ticks. Retained/restored and removed arms each make 68 incorrect admissions; conventional expanded makes 38. Neither stress predicate is acquired. Runtime scoring never revises the frozen expression. The learning closes one specific representation gap while leaving major residual coverage failures.

Live probes cover absent/stale trust, sparse ambiguous calibration, contradictory answers, removal of the successful candidate, corrupt validation, certificate alteration, deadlines, prices, source freshness, permission, query/refresh access and missed watches. A fresh exact-set audit can hold subsequent use after a residual model error. All actions are simulated. Identical enquiry plans are memoized for display speed; plan counters describe logical fresh construction. They omit some host work and do not measure total compute or elapsed time.

Through stage four, the sequence consumes stage three’s typed audited gap as an initiating calibration witness. Calibration and validation are freshly supplied development evidence; runtime observations and grants are new. Stage five replays the retained expression through an explicit transfer contract; its new teaching evidence and permissions are supplied separately. No relative-rate advantage is demonstrated.

## Fifth milestone: Extend How Repairs Are Built

This is a **new authored bounded study**, inspired by *The Aligned Signal* §18.4 (PDF p. 89) and the stronger developmental experiment proposed in *Constructive Enquiry* §10.2 (PDF p. 7). It is not a reproduction of a published meta-extensibility census.

Teacher A supplies 44 trusted measurements at n=4/5, c=3/4 for a response positive at `s=0` or `s=c`. Complete enumeration finds no fitting program in stage four’s G₀. A supplied three-recipe meta-grammar offers keep G₀, append pairwise AND or append pairwise OR. Only the OR constructor gives a unique fitting witness while preserving the declared contract. Its operative candidate language grows from 26 programs to 47: all 26 originals plus 21 unordered pairs of seven equality atoms. These are syntactic programs; they need not all denote distinct functions on a restricted context.

Qualification retains every old program, replays 312 protected denotations and checks four Boolean truth rows. The source witness is validated against 2,170 distinct n=6/c=3,4 measurements. Method selection charges 5,280 candidate response predictions across the three trials, including failed trials; source validation, preservation, artifact replay and runtime work are separate. A structural binding assumes the honest local host; it does not authenticate the measurement source.

Fresh Task B supplies another 44-answer calibration and separate 2,170-answer validation for `s=0 OR s=c−1`. The acquired recipe constructs a **different** expression. Checking fewer than all 47 candidates is `search-incomplete`, even if a survivor is already present. Complete G₀ search has no fitting B expression. The A/B measurements were inspected during development; B tests subsequent acquisition, not an untouched evaluation of the meta-selector. Neither acquisition uses final n=7 runtime outcomes.

At 48 unit-price ticks, all 70 n=7/c=3,4 new-task cases and 210 separate AND/XOR/OR legacy cases are enumerated:

| Constructor available before fresh B learning | New correct | New withheld | Legacy correct | Method acquisition rounds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fixed original | 0 | 70 | 210 | 0 |
| Acquired OR pairs | 70 | 0 | 210 | 1 |
| Removed after A, before B | 0 | 70 | 210 | 1 |
| Exact recipe restored | 70 | 0 | 210 | 1, plus replay |
| Preinstalled OR pairs | 70 | 0 | 210 | 0 |

Every arm has zero incorrect admissions and zero missed watches. Fixed/removed new-task holds follow an explicit **known unfilled B-model contract**; they are not wrong predictions or evidence that the original library would always misidentify K. Legacy outcomes remain correct, but expanded runtime work rises from 2,074 to 2,131 units. New-task expanded work is 690 units. Preinstallation also succeeds: the study establishes no conventional-baseline efficiency advantage or relative development-rate dominance.

Pause the constructor **after** B is retained and the expression stays usable under its retained decoder and own replay. Pause it **before fresh** B learning and new acquisition fails; restoring the checked recipe recovers it. Teaching and validation carry matching task contracts; an acquired A expression cannot fill B’s model contract. The live permission remains off until separately selected. Source qualification is replayed from stage four; old runtime observations and grants are not transferred.

The next boundary is explicit: `s∈{0,1,c}` has no fitting program among all 47 candidates across the teaching scope. A response-shape plot compares that target with the nearest admitted program and marks disagreement at the selected cardinality. The Boolean floor/ceiling are 0/1; new syntax remains limited to one join and two equality leaves. This identifies a language limit without automatically authorizing another growth step. Trust, full scope, preserved meanings, unchanged old syntax, validation, certificate replay, access, freshness, deadlines and continuing-duty probes all have separate holds.

Only the operative repair constructor changes. Boolean meanings, higher meta-grammar, meta-selector, qualifier and adoption rules remain supplied and fixed.

## Sixth milestone: What Does Better Checking Cost?

This first bounded resource experiment uses the same expanded 47-program language and Task B teaching scope for two learners. Fit-only requires a unique full fit; checked adoption additionally requires current trusted feedback, conservative old-meaning preservation, separate validation and actual replay. The adoption obligations differ deliberately; this is not a benchmark of equally qualified learners or full MeRSIA rate dominance.

At unit weights, fit-only becomes available at 2,068 declared units. Checked adoption costs 316 preservation/truth checks + 2,068 fitting predictions + 2,170 validation predictions + 4,238 replay predictions = 8,792. Both frozen valid expressions pass all 8,820 n=7/c=3,4 Task B response checks. Wrong A teaching under the B contract yields a fitted model with only 5,250/8,820 correct responses; checked validation holds it. Untrusted/stale teaching, corrupt validation and removal of an old program have their own checked holds, unaffected by more budget.

Plots reconstruct availability prefixes from actual phase ledgers; they are not physical process suspensions or elapsed-time measurements. Validation and preservation weights can each be 1,2,4. Common source-method acquisition costs 8,394 default units and can be shown separately or charged equally to both arms. Fixture generation, scope/equality overhead, support rebuilding, memory and hardware costs are excluded; evaluation is separate. No faster acquisition, total-compute advantage or general alignment guarantee is established.

## Scope

Stages one–four are educational reconstructions of the worked encounter, qualified policy reuse, constructive enquiry and acquired response expressions. Stages five, six and seven are new bounded constructor-acquisition, resource-comparison and changed-obligation studies. These are not reproductions of the complete paper packages. Milestone two has two receiving contexts and four target requests; milestone three has twelve represented cases and six separate OR stress cases. The zero/two-error controls and audit stress bench in milestone one are exploratory variations. Rule languages, initial construction grammar, primitive meanings, higher meta-grammar, enumeration, qualifiers, audit fixtures and permissions are supplied. Stage five selects a bounded operative construction-language extension within that fixed foundation. The code assumes an honest local host. Equality is currentness, not authentication or hostile-process isolation. All actions are simulated; there are no external effects.

Milestone two implements bounded acquisition and inheritance of a construction procedure within a fixed grammar. Broader MeRSIA and relative-rate dominance remain research targets. Stage five implements conservative finite constructor growth and subsequent acquisition. Milestone four learns a response expression by finite program selection from audited examples. No milestone trains a neural model, invents semantic primitives, changes the higher meta-grammar or establishes a general alignment guarantee.

## Seventh milestone: When the Obligations Change

The old reference asks a count-level risk question `r₀(s,c)=1[s=0 or s=c−1]`. The externally authorized new reference asks `r₁(s,c)=1[s=1 or s=c]` and release eligibility `Q₁=¬r₁ ∧ permission ∧ anchor ∧ current`. The supplied adapter preserves the count meanings of the earlier equality-pair grammar; it is not a learned schema interpreter.

Replay the acquired OR constructor carried through stage six. Nine teaching records at c=3,4 select a different expression, `(s=1) OR (s=c)`; six separate records at c=5 validate it. Search all 32 coordinate projections over 120 records at c=3..5. The coarse `(s,c)` view has a concrete collision witness; within this coordinate language all five fields are necessary. Rule-only coverage is 64/272; repaired view coverage is 272/272 in the frozen c=6..9 census. With supplied current receiving grants: 26 correct releases, 246 blocks, zero invalid releases. The unchecked old-rule shortcut would make 190 invalid releases. All effects are simulated.

Later-work cost is 4,303 units for inherited, restored and preinstalled OR routes; direct enumeration over base plus AND/OR pairs costs 4,492. The latter also succeeds. The 189-unit difference is conditional candidate pruning, not rate dominance or superiority over every optimized comparator. Actual source acquisition/replay is shown separately: 8,394 per pass; standalone inheritance runs two passes, restoration three. A sequence run uses one stage-seven replay of the forwarded artifact, excluding earlier stages’ work from this later task. Qualification visits, prediction counts and historical replay are charged; hardware, elapsed time, data construction, encoding and frozen scoring are not.

Removing the constructor leaves no expression in the base grammar. Old answers mislabeled as new teaching fail separate validation. A three-peak target exceeds the one-pair grammar. Unauthorized reference changes and swapped field mappings remain held. Known renamed fields require a supplied bijection and 120 correspondence checks. Receiving permission starts off. A changed revision invalidates the qualification receipt even if field values return. Currentness is exact host binding, not authentication. Historical checks retain the old reference separately; a new goal is not a better score on the old goal.

Download `dist/downloads/07_changing_obligations.py`. The native reference reconstructs the constructor acquisition locally and matches all declared numerical interventions. Full cross-language receipt import is not claimed. This is authored finite verification, not independent scientific replication, autonomous normative self-governance, or a generalized MeRSIA result.
